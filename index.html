Kanji Key

Write a kanji to open your apps.

Kanji Key turns the moment you reach for social media into a kanji lesson. Pick the apps you open too often. Each time you open one, write a kanji from memory, and it opens. Lessons and reviews follow the JLPT, from N5 to N1, and come back right when you would forget.

For learners of Japanese. The app, kanji meanings and example words are available in 44 languages.
