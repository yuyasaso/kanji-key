Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to taurus36y@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

How does the lock work?

When you open an app you picked, Kanji Key shows a lock screen. Tap the button, write the kanji, and the app opens for a while (15, 10 or 5 minutes, depending on how much help you needed). You can change the apps, the number of kanji and the time in the Lock tab.

How do I stop the lock?

Open the Lock tab and remove the apps, or turn off Screen Time access for Kanji Key in Settings › Screen Time. Deleting the app also removes the lock.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions › Kanji Key. You can also open it from Kanji Key: Settings › Manage subscription. If you cancel during the free week, you are not charged.

I changed phones. How do I get my progress back?

If you signed in with Apple on your old phone, sign in with the same Apple Account in Settings on the new one. Your subscription comes back with Restore purchases. The apps to lock have to be picked again on each phone.

How do I delete my account?

In Kanji Key, go to Settings › Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
