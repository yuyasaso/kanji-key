Kanji Key

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Kanji Key is an iPhone app that asks you to write a Japanese kanji before you open the apps you choose. This policy explains what data the app uses and where it goes. In short: almost everything stays on your iPhone. We do not show ads, we do not track you, and we do not sell data.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Your learning progress: the kanji you have learned, your answers, review dates, XP, streak, goal and language.
  • Your lock settings: which apps to lock, how many kanji to write, and for how long apps open.
  • Screen Time: the app uses Apple's Screen Time framework to lock the apps you pick and to show how much time you spend on social media. Apple only gives the app anonymous tokens for the apps you pick, so we cannot see which apps they are. Your usage time is shown by an Apple system extension and never leaves your iPhone. We cannot see it.

We have no access to this data. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Optional: Sign in with Apple

You can use Kanji Key without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple to keep your progress when you change phones:

  • We receive an anonymous user identifier from Apple. We do not ask for your name or email address.
  • We store a copy of your learning progress and lock settings (not the apps you lock) on our server, so it can be restored on a new iPhone.
  • We store a token from Apple that lets us revoke your Sign in with Apple authorization when you delete your account.

The server is run by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Only you can read your data in the app. We use it only to sync your progress.

Usage statistics

To improve the app, Kanji Key sends usage statistics to our server: which steps of the setup and the subscription screen you reached and how long they took, lessons and reviews you finished (number of kanji, accuracy, XP), lock events (when a lock screen was used, a kanji was written, an app was unlocked or locked again), your settings (such as the number of kanji to write and how many apps are locked, not which apps), whether Screen Time and notifications are allowed, your subscription status, errors, and crash and freeze reports from iOS.

Each event comes with the app version, iOS version, iPhone model, region and language, and a random identifier that is created when you install the app. This identifier is not your Apple ID, name, email or advertising identifier, and it is not linked to your account. It is deleted when you delete the app, and it is sent without your account, so we do not link these statistics to you. We keep them for at most 25 months and use them only to understand and improve the app, not for advertising, and we do not share them with anyone. The apps you lock and your Screen Time usage never leave your iPhone.

Requesting a language

If Kanji Key is not in your language yet, you can send a request. We store only the country you picked and the language name you typed. This is not linked to you or your device.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings › Delete account. This deletes your account and all data on our server, and revokes the app's Sign in with Apple authorization. Your progress on your iPhone stays until you delete the app.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Children

Kanji Key is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to taurus36y@gmail.com. If you are in the EU, you can also contact your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
